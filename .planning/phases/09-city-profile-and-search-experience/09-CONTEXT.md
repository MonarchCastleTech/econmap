# Phase 9 Context

## Goal

Every canonical city must open a usable dossier even when no sparse enrichment file exists.

## Decisions

- Registry-only profiles include identity, hierarchy, coordinates, observed population, and source
  lineage.
- Optional observations are additive.
- Missing enrichments are visible as queued, missing, or unknown gaps.
- Subordinate places remain in the source registry for identity and disambiguation but are excluded
  from the public city result set and city coverage totals.
